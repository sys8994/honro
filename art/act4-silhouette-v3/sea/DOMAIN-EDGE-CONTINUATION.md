# Native edge continuation outside the playable art

Browser minimum zoom exposed a substantial vertical material cut on both Stage31 margins. The four old full-domain bodies already had the correct `collisionSource`; this was not a role-classification fault. Their low-resolution strata were roughly four times deeper than the high-resolution play artwork and met it at a hard clipping edge.

`register-domain-edges.py` reads the exact native filled paths at each playable source edge, computes boundary intersections and their relative world depths, and continues those material bands along the frozen far-bank terrain. It replaces only four off-play paint instances with two small technical continuation assets. It does not invent a decorative contour, alter physical geometry, recolor gameplay artwork, add noise, add an opacity veil, or change the engine. Original concepts, native traces and previous bodies remain preserved. All terrain and original play vector objects remain byte-equivalent; `domain-edge-parity.json` records hashes.

The same mismatch existed structurally in31/37/40, so all three have isolated candidates. Stage32 already uses edge-reflected, support-aligned copies of the same native play artwork for its margins, rather than a separately scaled low-resolution material; it is intentionally unchanged.

Native intersections exclude the artificial source-image bottom. Consecutive same-color layers merge without changing visible paint. The resulting continuation uses5 paths per edge, follows every frozen far contour vertex, and extends to the actual physical bottom. These are quiet off-play technical fills, not additional gameplay assets.
