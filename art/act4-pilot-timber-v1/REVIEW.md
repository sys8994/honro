# Thin one-way timber native candidates

Root generated source and actually traced Colors8/stack/removebg/default2/1/.2.
Four whole native contours groups extracted; no clipping, source immutable.
Root reviewed the top board envelope and explicit endpoint domains. Broken ends
are decorative non-walkable overhangs, dark navy braces are rear structure rather
than solid gameplay walls. Exact matched Engine motion still requires review:
passing through a thin one-way board differs from the rejected deep rock body.

Rebuild with `extract-semantic-contours.py`, then native-union.mjs per raw.svg,
`refine-body-contact.py art/act4-pilot-timber-v1`, quantize .005 and import-svg.py
per asset-config.json. No palette revision applied to timber. The original source
and actual native trace have slightly different palette/edge behavior; final
normal/max-zoom art and contact acceptance are pending.

The whole-stage assembler `--stage-specific` reads these modules and the three
stage-specific BODY modules. It never substitutes rejected common rock bodies;
shore is explicitly absent until a separately approved low-bank asset exists.
Timber's response to multi-facet target tops must be visually reviewed; bent
board shapes are not accepted solely because their contact mapping is exact.
