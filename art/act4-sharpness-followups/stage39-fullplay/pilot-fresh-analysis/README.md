# Thirty-one-turn run delay analysis

`rounds.md` is generated from the recorded headless pilot-combat run. Each row is the first and last sampled position/support/movement remainder for an active hero in the round, plus predicted chosen attack targets and explicitly logged movement commands. Engine actions can reset the move budget (especially post-shot retreat), and the last sample can precede settling; the columns are not a continuous trajectory or a measure of AP actually spent.

The knight alone moved from x405 at turn 1 to x4616 by turn 8, completing net and anchor by turn 9. The mage remained around x1025–1324 from turns 6–19; the occultist remained around x1129–1323 over the same span. They repeatedly chose western/upper targets, including threats on different elevations, while large movement budgets remained at the first samples. The archer reached x4676 by turn 15 but kept attacking distant threats even after residents were safe in turn 10. The mage briefly moved west in turns 19–29 and both delayed heroes reached the exit only on turns 30–31. This accounts for much of the 21-turn interval between rescue and completion and is a controller strategy/targeting failure, not evidence of a level impossibility.

Two knight HP drops during the low-strand/high-tide traversal match the game's normal water recovery behavior; the log does not capture a standalone water-recovery detail object, so the exact water accounting is limited to sampled position/HP. The optional wreck also died from actual collateral damage although it was not a chosen target. The next policy must move the group together before offensive actions, prefer supported high ground during rising/high tide, and switch to evacuation as soon as rescue is secure. Normal UI inputs, health and mission conditions remain unchanged. The whole prior run and 20-turn failure remain preserved separately.
