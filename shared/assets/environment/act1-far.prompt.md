# Act 1 distant backdrop

Current source and runtime asset (HBUG-072): [`act1-far.svg`](act1-far.svg), a hand-authored 1600×900 pure vector drawing. Named groups separate the sky/moon, receding ridges, large granite planes, overlapping spurs, valley clouds and lower mist. It contains no embedded raster, external references, filters or scripts. The shared build embeds this SVG in both game and Workshop; Canvas uses the existing image drawing path with aspect-preserving cover cropping. The portrait view prioritizes the central mountains and may crop out the moon.

The interrupted conversation had completed HBUG-071 with raster artwork and integration, but had not produced the SVG requested in the resumed conversation. Those files are retained as historical artwork and are no longer bundled. `act1-far-master.png` was generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool from the user's reference screenshot, then edited once to remove the stray waterfalls. The screenshot was used only for its distant mountains, clouds, sky, moon, palette, and atmosphere. `act1-far.webp` is its quality-88 WebP conversion.

Generate a NEW wide 16:9 background image with NO foreground. Palette: deep blue-black sky, desaturated blue-gray granite, muted blue-green shadows, pale gray mist, soft ivory moon. Moody, majestic, atmospheric, painterly, refined and cohesive, with strong large-scale composition that remains legible at 25% size. Absolutely no foreground trees, no pine silhouettes, no close rocks, no cliffs framing the edges, no ground, no water, no lake, no waterfall, no temple or buildings, no characters, no signs, no text. No flat polygon art, no simple gradients, no low-poly rendering. Distant vista only.
