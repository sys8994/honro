# Stage 36: A02 and lantern tactics comparison

One same-context Chromium run with the nine-enemy fixed 4ebe candidate completed combat in **7 turns / 26 accepted shots** and the mission in **8 turns**, with 9/9 enemies defeated and all four party members alive. The prior control-corrected comparison took combat 8 / mission 9 turns and 29 accepted shots, with a real UI export between two contexts. Thus this observed run used 1 fewer combat turn, 1 fewer total turn and 3 fewer shots; it does not establish an average, probability or a difficulty pass. The original representative 11/13-turn run lacked per-action state outputs and is not silently replaced.

The [policy was recorded before play](TACTICS-36-POLICY.md). No game code, live HP, enemy, position, receipt or clear flag was edited. Collision records and HP intervals are retained in the action stream rather than invented from screenshots.

The visible lantern was prioritized with equipped spirit O02. After its O02 shots at turns 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6, respectively 291, 300, 306, 326 and 24 lantern HP loss was observed across the subsequent resolution intervals; the last interval ended in its defeat on turn 6. A mage M06 shot at it on turn 5 had no observed enemy HP reduction by the next player actor. These are time-window observations; overlapping enemy actions or persistent effects can make individual causation uncertain.

The two comparisons each removed the same initial 15,354 enemy HP. As a coarse run-level ratio, that is 590.5 initial HP per accepted shot for this 26-shot run versus 529.4 for the 29-shot run (11.5% higher); this is not projectile damage, a hit rate, or a per-shot causal attribution. Using the recorded interval from an accepted shot until the next player actor, this run had 3 intervals without observed enemy HP loss. The separately reviewed 29-shot log had 5 such intervals, including delayed damage after 10 shots whose immediate sample showed none. Intervals can contain overlapping events. The new run's 26 accepted shots were archer 7, mage 7, knight 6, occultist 6; skill counts A02 4, A14 3, M06 7, S06 6, O03 1, O02 5. Four normal jump inputs and two defensive rotations were recorded. The 207 controller entries include waits, captures and UI commands and are not 207 attacks.

DOOR.01/.02 and EXIT.01/.02 were observed. All four walked the ground exit path with no negative HP delta during the recorded post-combat moves; after reaching x>3212 they used normal E and the game showed [victory](evidence/tactics36/victory.webp). Final HP: archer 1766, mage 1240.2, knight 3434, occultist 2102.2; defensive healing is included. The displayed 28:57 includes observation/debug waits and is not active human play time. The bounded original action log is [here](evidence/tactics36/README.md); shots, misses and HP causes are not reconstructed where not observed.

These results demonstrate that the current loadout can make useful piercing contacts and that a deliberate spirit attack can remove the lantern before the prior run's late aerial chase. They do not prove that the nine-enemy layout meets the original scene's intended pacing, nor quantify the share of the earlier delay attributable to controls alone. The separate proposal to compare an original-design two-cluster layout was not applied to this run.
