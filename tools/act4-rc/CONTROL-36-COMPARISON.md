## Controller preflight required before a new run

Confirm the original context remains controllable through its actual Playwright page; keep PTY/stdin open and verify command roundtrip. Confirm the driver implements and rejects unsupported commands explicitly, and verify normal pointer/`wheel` zoom in both directions before starting combat; compare the actual camera scale before, after zoom-out, and after zoom-in, with combat state unchanged. The optional `HONRO_PREFLIGHT=1` mode performs that camera check on a disposable copy of the initial save and exits. On this build the measured scale was 0.94 → 0.46679 → 0.94 with round, units, HP, RNG, shots and kills unchanged. Also preflight actual UI export/download, read-only raw snapshot, actual `page.reload()` and Continue, and preserve their distinct provenance; a controller that merely exposes a menu or accepts an unknown command has not passed. Observe the active character, remaining movement, footing and line of sight before firing; move the rest of the party forward when their actions are available. A 100-observation watchdog diagnoses lack of progress and is not an automatic mission failure.
