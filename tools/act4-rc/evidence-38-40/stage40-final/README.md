# Stage 40: fresh UI run on the fixed common RC

On 2026-10-31 a fresh actual Chromium UI run cleared stage 40 in **14 turns**, with all four heroes and all four residents alive, all four enemies defeated, and all required return dialogue shown. This is one observed run, not an average or probability. The fight ended in turn 11: it exceeded the intended 2–4-turn opening even with a west/east party split. Several missed or poorly selected attacks and defensive actions contributed, so this run does not isolate enemy balance as the cause. The previous 9-turn spur stall on the older source remains a separate failed/interrupted run, not this result.

## Combat and escort

Archer and occultist fought the western hound and guard, while the knight climbed east for the two archers; the mage repositioned to keep allies out of the line of fire and to prepare the western trigger. Skills observed include archer A05, mage M06, occultist O01/O02 and knight S00. Controlled 400 ms Space holds produced actual logged powers around 22–33%; no power was assigned directly. Mage M06 missed in turn 1. In turn 6 the upper enemy moved behind the knight before a directional S00 and the shot missed; an incorrect Tab selection also caused a useless archer shot from the boat. The knight's subsequent shots missed at roughly 159 horizontal separation or on a steep height difference. The last upper archer was finished in turn 11; the mage already stood in the west boarding trigger, so the knight did not have to return west.

Both boarding rows were visibly presented and individually advanced. Two residents reached the high route, while two walked aboard `approved40:ferry`; the ferry sailed and landed before the passengers walked and jumped up the spur. All four reached distinct safe slots without the old stall. In this observed order the high-route residents occupied x4840 and x4760, followed by the passengers at x4680 and x4600. This single run does not prove every possible arrival order. Escort clock at safe was approximately 131.3 game seconds, including dialog, UI export, screen capture, tool latency and inspection; it is not an unaided play-time measurement.

The boat carried the archer on its support surface. On the first attempt to climb after landing, a long jump missed the coast and caused a water recovery to the boat. Water recovery reported 457 HP loss; total HP fell from 1825 to 1255 including the fall. Subsequent ordinary short jumps climbed the route; round recovery restored archer HP to 1328 by final interaction. This is a controller failure and demonstrates a real fall cost, not a no-damage route. At the final interaction the heroes had HP 1328, 1978, 3165 and 2039; all residents had 1450/1450. Outcome UI reported 14 turns, 4 kills, 0 allied incapacitations. Its 30:15 active combat time includes substantial interactive debugging/observation and is not a valid human play-time estimate.

The displayed BOARD.01–02, REUNION.01–02, THIRTEEN.01–03 and NEXT.01–04 rows were individually advanced after presentation; all 11 appear in `presented`, none in `retired`. The final `eq_so_t3b` fact includes roster total 13, living 11 and prior deaths 2. E at the grounded four-hero finish produced the victory outcome and campaign-ending rest UI.
