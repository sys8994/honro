The failed replay trace narrows the tactical issue: at round 9 the knight had 197 HP but moved from approximately x4856 to x5194 and used `S06`; it was incapacitated before its next turn. In round 11 the occultist had 149 HP and used `O02`, then was incapacitated before round 12. The existing policy selected attacks without an explicit low-HP survival decision. This supports a controller/strategy concern, but does not by itself prove that a safer reachable action would have survived the exact enemy sequence. Diagnose reachable cover, defense, healing and forecast before using another fresh run; do not weaken enemies or call the result a pure difficulty failure.
