# Stage 39 observer analysis rules (pre-registered)

Apply these rules unchanged to both final v6 traces. The goal is to distinguish real route pressure from enemies that do not meaningfully affect the party, without equating kills or visible placement with engagement.

* A completed enemy action is one unique `(run, entry round, enemy actor ID)` with `finishAction`, actor side 1, entry side 1, and `reviewed=false`. Further calls for that same tuple, including the review-completion call, are not additional completed actions. Also report the raw calls and duplicates. A finished action by itself does not imply pressure.
* The primary attributable hostile-damage sample is a `hurt` record with HP or shield loss, an owner ID present in the initial enemy roster, a known skill, `source=normal`, a hero or resident target, and owner distinct from target. A damage event is not a projectile hit and is not necessarily an entire attack. The event's actual HP loss and shield loss are reported separately, and HP loss / the victim's known max HP gives an event-level HP-loss fraction. Max HP comes from the entry snapshot for heroes; resident maximum is used only if explicitly present in a game record, otherwise the fraction is unknown. A shield-only hit is a zero-HP-loss sample, not a zero-damage result. The input `attempted` is pre-mitigation and is not an attack-level denominator.
* List and count separately all other positive HP/shield-loss records: unknown/non-roster owner, unknown skill, environment/non-normal source, same-owner/target, friendly fire and damage to enemies. Where categories overlap, show flags rather than treating their counts as disjoint. Healing is not captured by `hurt`; snapshot HP increases are not damage or a causally attributed healing event.
* Narrow direct exchange: unique enemy IDs either supplying a primary attributable hostile-damage event or receiving a positive HP/shield-loss `hurt` from a known hero owner. Counts are unique enemies, never kills. Include ID lists and overlap.
* Additional direct pressure: unique enemies supplying an actual `impact` whose unit target is a hero or resident, with owner in the initial enemy roster, excluding already counted narrow exchange. Report enemy IDs, target IDs and whether any primary attributable loss followed. Impact establishes a collision, not its intent, damage or player perception.
* Enemy fire without a traced target, terrain-only impacts, noncolliding projectiles, potential firing lines and distant placement do not qualify as confirmed pressure. A pressure decision from a direct collision/occlusion qualifies only with an actual collision or block AND a linked input/screenshot demonstrating that a player changed course or selected a different action because of it; absent a causal record, label it unknown. Do not claim off-screen fire targets the party. Summaries of these unknowns are not added to the direct counts.
* The union of narrow exchange and additional direct-pressure IDs is an intentionally conservative lower-bound observation of direct interactions in the trace, not necessarily the project's undefined “effective engagement” measure. Do not declare the 18–26 criterion met or missed from this surrogate alone. Explain which evidence, such as target-resolved non-damaging fire, threat exposure or linked decisions, is missing.
* Pilot warning, visible reaction opportunity, evasion and escort-defense choice are separate. Screenshot evidence establishes a displayed frame; paired actual input/turn records establish a response opportunity, and actual effect or protection must be separately demonstrated. Never infer successful evasion from warning receipt alone.

The two runs are headless Chromium, the same explicit fixture and game-created seed with different fixed policies. Observation overhead and real-time scheduling can alter timing. These are not a human win-rate experiment, an independent blind playtest, or proof of causal strategy superiority. Prior v1–v5 records remain unchanged.
