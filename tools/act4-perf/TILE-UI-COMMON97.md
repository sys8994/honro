## Common-source timing and remaining tile cost

The same synthetic stage-40 direct App entry and six-second real dialogue fixture as `INCREMENTAL-GUIDE.md` was run against frozen-world QA builds. One 97fa baseline and two view-guard candidates are not a statistically conclusive or exclusive-host comparison. Baseline first water was 589.6ms; candidates 352.8/375.6ms. Ensure-stage through first actual render was 1672.5ms baseline and 1913.9/1827.9ms candidate; this is not evidence of a load-time improvement. Guide rAF median/p95/max was 16.7/16.8/100.0ms baseline and 16.7/16.8/83.4, 16.7/16.7/83.4ms candidate; longest observed idle callback 1.7ms versus 9.5/4.9ms. Input-to-handler observations were 2.6 versus 2.3/10.6ms and include Playwright/protocol delay. The 20 fixed water-frame RGBA hashes matched. A 300ms early-input candidate cancelled unfinished work and its first water draw was 617.1ms. These are headless PC/DPR1 samples, not phone FPS or a release gate.

One separately instrumented earlier QA run (frozen world, 4ebe+compact+e6 before c1) measured a 373.4ms first water render after preparation. Nested layer wrappers saw 12 mid-landmark calls totalling 9.2ms and structural calls 4.0ms; Canvas `drawImage` took 343.2ms across 13 calls, maximum 51.7ms. Timings are nested/instrumented and not additive. Chromium/Skia may defer rasterization and flush it inside `drawImage`, so this does not prove pure image-compositing cost. Animated water is already painted by `liveWater` after the static world cache; no dynamic water was folded into the tile cache. The real restarted guide's current view already had 15 cached tiles after first render. On closing the dialogue the observed automatic view changed from scale .72 to .94 and needed another raster key. The later water camera in the fixed probe is a diagnostic camera position, not an established next player camera.

An isolated scratch-canvas probe recorded the exact water-view tiles and compared direct `drawImage` versus `createImageBitmap`. In separate single runs the first direct draw blocked 46.5ms; the first `createImageBitmap` call itself blocked 42.9ms before its promise was returned. Subsequent calls were near zero in those runs; record phase was 241ms versus 20.3ms respectively, showing strong noise/deferred-work uncertainty. It does not establish a safe per-tile budget or a performance win. We did not pre-render a guessed camera, lower resolution, extend the guide, or adopt the bitmap approach. A true bounded tile/off-main-thread solution and its source/clip/pixel/cancellation validation remain open; ~350–440ms first-water stalls remain in the guide-prepared candidate.
