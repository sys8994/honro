# Speculative chapter prewarm candidate

Independent performance candidate based on RC `0282ac196`; this is not a production build or gameplay/release approval. The entry-path and unit observations below do not establish a mobile-device result. The QA build HTML SHA-256 was `411de25ba010ac9b67e26bd25319d6341f7034d890849bf8176341f2e1bc8135`.

The previous title prewarm schedules immediately on idle and uses the required loader's request/worker-parse cache. In a headless Chromium stage 39 fixture, its stage 40 descriptor started at 2.083 s and parts continued after battle started at 5.400 s. Under the candidate, diagnostic `HonroApp.launch(39)` entered battle at 2.701 s and no chapter 40 requests occurred over the subsequent 10 s. On a separate candidate title left idle, the descriptor began after the three-second delay, all three parts and the stage JSON completed, and stage 40 remained unregistered (`has(40) == false`). The two timing samples experienced different host load and are not an FPS comparison. The test launches through the real App method with a declared entry profile; it is not proof of full 39 play or normal title-button behavior. The raw network event files are in `evidence/prewarm-browser.tar.xz`, SHA-256 `0aa0ab0fbf121c08d3634dd3ae875e31057517a5fe1bf4bc9cefe68d4e6adf1a`.

This candidate waits three seconds then an idle callback, fetches next-stage bytes sequentially at low priority without parsing/hydrating them, and cancels its independent abort controller when a required stage, battle mount, hidden page or pagehide makes the speculative work inappropriate. It never cancels a required request. `node tests/act4-prewarm-scheduler.mjs` passes delayed-entry cancellation, idle warming, and cancellation of an in-flight part followed by a successful required load. Those latter cancellation assertions use a controlled mock, not real network throttling. This relies on the browser's HTTP cache rather than keeping a parsed prefetch in memory: servers without a usable cache may redownload later, so next-entry latency and bandwidth need a real hosting-header test. Normal title UI, real in-flight abort, background/visibility changes and all campaign/workshop entry regressions remain gates before integration. Do not weaken the release guard or deploy this QA build.


A follow-up used actual title and stage-entry buttons with CDP bandwidth capped at 180 kB/s. The speculative stage 40 part began, then failed with `net::ERR_ABORTED` after the stage-entry click. The first attempt clicked during the rest UI input lock and produced no transition; the second waited 700 ms and reached the loading indicator, but did not reach battle in 15 seconds under the cap. The screenshot shows that the declared fixture led to **stage 31** rest, not stage 39: do not interpret this UI observation as normal 39 entry. The requested load target and whether it succeeds with throttling remain unverified. Logs and the genuine rest/loading screenshot are preserved in `evidence/prewarm-slow-ui.tar.xz`; no HP/progress was changed to obtain this result.
