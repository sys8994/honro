## Method and limits

`entry_events.py` launches the real story entry with a prepared earlier-clears profile, dismisses dialogue, allows a short settle, freezes and captures. All units/terrain remain; actor and dynamic landmark lists are saved. It does not activate later triggers, simulate combat completion, or validate physical devices/FPS. `layer_compare.py` temporarily suppresses draw calls, not source data; it is only for causal visual isolation. 34/38 direct mode calls the same scene landmark/terrain drawing functions without the world raster cache; it is a diagnostic, not a production path. Neither comparison proves equivalence to the original pre-trace artwork.
