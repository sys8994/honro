## What is not established

- No numeric human fun rating, player-clear probability, or validated global1→6 difficulty curve is claimed.
- No raw jump quota is imposed. Recorded matched landings/support IDs and sampled displacement describe actual travel; meaningful tactical elevation still requires replay review.
- A large initial roster is not simultaneous combat. Required-route engagement attribution, effective enemy actions and miniboss pattern/counterplay gates remain unvalidated rather than being inferred from total kills or labels.
- Current useful evidence identifies route, protection, support, worksite and physical-zone choices. Recovery pacing, understandable feedback and whether those choices are enjoyable require actual browser/human observation.
- All original required thresholds are retained. Missing evidence is fail-closed; reference counts are not promoted into invented mandatory minima.
