# Stage 6 pursuit QA (2026-11-03)

Status: **prototype, not release accepted**. The new valley geometry remains a cloned compiled-map overlay; it is not the published authored map or finished art. Do not use these results as approval to deploy.

The fresh-battle-only pursuit warns in the objective area, shows an estimated position behind the moving carrier, and waits for a real allied action after the warning has appeared. At the transition after allied movement it attempts atomic, safe placement; it does not relocate existing enemies, increase the enemy action cap, add a kill quota, or hold the exit open. Old saved battles do not acquire the new revision. The original goals and save boundary remain unchanged. The camera-relative arrow is an estimate, not an exact promise of a spawn point.
